Binomial Distribution

The Binomial distribution models the number of successes in a fixed number of independent trials, with the same probability of success on every trial.

P(X = k) = C(n, k) pk (1 − p)n−k

CDF cumulative probability calculator

Calculate the probability of k or fewer successes, and the probability of more than k successes.

P(X ≤ k)
—

P(X ≤ k) means the probability of getting k or fewer successes.

P(X > k)
—

This is calculated as 1 − P(X ≤ k).
